Third-party notices for Renderer/
==================================

The Metal shaders in this folder, the camera (MetalCamera.swift, MetalUserCamera.swift) and the
draw ordering (MetalRenderer.swift) are hand ports of the Khronos glTF Sample Renderer, pinned
commit 863b981 (https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Renderer, Apache-2.0, see
LICENSE.md). The lighting bakes in ../Environments were exported from that renderer; see
../Environments/LICENSES.md for the environment images they are made from.
